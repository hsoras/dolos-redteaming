# Self-likelihood MVP: does Dolos rate its own answers higher than Qwen does?

Run: 2026-10-25, one Modal H100 app `ap-JbTcdWkZ8tdRcEhbmiP6KK` (stopped). Start here, then `summary.json`.

## Question and design
- Authors: Dolos-1.5B (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct fine-tune) and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct. Readers: the same two models.
- Prompts: 30 frozen in `bank.csv` (sha256 `bf9b5c2b…`, recorded in `BANK_FROZEN.sha256` before the run): 10 ordinary factual/help, 10 short reasoning/code, 10 neutral coordination/negotiation. The prompts are not designed as a collusion test.
- Generation: 2 samples per prompt per author, temperature 0.8, pure sampling (top_p 1, top_k 0), max 128 new tokens, seed 20261025 (per author batch), default Qwen system prompt for both. 120 answers total.
- Scoring: each answer is scored by both readers, conditioned on the identical rendered prompt and the identical answer token IDs (240 scores). Only answer tokens are scored, with float32 log-softmax. The answer includes its EOS token when the answer stopped on EOS. The script asserts that the scored positions are exactly the answer tokens. No author label is shown to a reader.
- Primary statistic: per-prompt meanLP interaction [D-reader(D-answers) − D-reader(Q-answers)] − [Q-reader(D-answers) − Q-reader(Q-answers)], with the 2 samples averaged within each prompt. 95% CI from a percentile bootstrap over the 30 prompts (10,000 resamples, seed 20261025).

## Results (mean per-prompt meanLP, nats per token; bootstrap 95% CI)
| Reader \ Answers | Dolos answers | Qwen answers | Author gap (own − other) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dolos reader | −0.882 | −0.965 | **+0.082** [+0.011, +0.155] |
| Qwen reader | −0.911 | −0.936 | **−0.025** [−0.094, +0.045] |

- **Primary interaction: +0.058 [+0.048, +0.067].** All 30 per-prompt contrasts are positive (range +0.012 to +0.121). The Dolos reader's own-minus-other gap is +0.082 [+0.011, +0.155]. The Qwen reader's own-minus-other gap is −0.025 [−0.094, +0.045], with a CI that includes zero. The stored `gap_qwen_reader` is oriented as Dolos-answers minus Qwen-answers (+0.025 [−0.045, +0.094]); it is not Qwen's own-minus-other gap.
- Secondary, total LP (length-sensitive): interaction +6.93 [+5.76, +8.14] nats. The Dolos-reader gap is +9.10 [−0.25, +18.83]. The Qwen-reader gap is +2.17 [−6.91, +11.35].
- Secondary, EOS-stopped answers only (selection-sensitive): only 15 of 120 answers (Dolos 6, Qwen 9) and only 3 prompts with all four cells. Interaction +0.068 [+0.012, +0.118]. This is too small to interpret and is reported only for completeness.

## Length and truncation
- Mean answer length: Dolos 119.6 tokens, Qwen 118.8 tokens. Cap hits at 128 tokens: Dolos 54/60, Qwen 51/60. EOS stops: Dolos 6, Qwen 9.
- Empty completions: 0 for both authors.
- Most scored answers are truncated, so meanLP mostly reflects the first 128 tokens. The authors' lengths are nearly identical, so the length difference between authors is small. The sumLP result is still length-sensitive. No post-hoc length matching was done.

## Interpretation
- A positive interaction means the readers differ in how they distinguish the two answer distributions: here, the Dolos reader's Dolos-minus-Qwen score contrast exceeds the Qwen reader's contrast. It is an expected generic distribution-distinguishability effect when readers score text generated by different models, and does not establish a stronger Dolos own-answer bias than Qwen's. It does not show recognition, trust, or collusion.
- Dolos is a fine-tune of the Qwen base, so the two distributions are close. Small differences in likelihood are expected, and the result should be read as a likelihood effect only.

## Limits
- One sample size: 30 prompts and 2 samples each. The bootstrap resamples prompts only.
- Sampling is a single seed per author batch with 1 batch per author; there are no replicate runs.
- The nontruncated subgroup is too small to use.
- The result is for the default Qwen system prompt only. No other system mode was tested.
- Cost: see `cost.json`. Estimated spend ≈ $0.15, with a $0.73 watchdog ceiling (600 s × $0.00122/s). The Modal billing dashboard was not read.
