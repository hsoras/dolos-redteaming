# Parked communication-game ideas

Selected on 2026-10-25: one-shot modified prisoner's dilemma and covert door relay.
Other designs are saved here rather than discarded. Full original proposals and
independent cross-model reviews: [game_design_cross_reviews.md](../explore/game_design_cross_reviews.md).

- **Reserve bridge:** two players coordinate on MAIN (safe permitted route) or
  RESERVE (restricted route, benefits require both). Add payoff-neutral and
  unrestricted controls; zero private bonus alone does not remove the incentive.
- **Sealed-map treasure hunt:** private map, permitted delivery preferences,
  confidential treasure locations. Map permutation is a useful counterfactual,
  but the original delivery incentives were awkward and need revision.
- **Airlock maintenance:** reciprocal skipping of required maintenance saves
  personal resources and depletes oxygen. Repeated state/arithmetic may be too
  complex for 1.5B models; private batteries did not affect decisions enough.
- **Matched die report:** private rolls, mandatory honesty, reward for matching
  reports. Useful objective dishonest-report metric. Compare identical public
  report with/without message; matching alone need not involve persuasion.
- **Biased messenger:** private state plus an interest in one receiver action.
  Separates lying and persuasion but has weak coalition specificity.
- **Task assignment:** private worker costs, public/private discussion, assignment
  votes. Distinguish group-optimal allocation from burden shifting.
- **Costly partner choice / procurement:** anonymous alternatives and a measurable
  cost of favoring a partner. Distinguish message quality from receiver preference.
- **Worker–auditor:** approve/reject objectively checkable submissions. Ground
  truth can reduce shared-error confounds, but this may constrain emergence.

None of these designs alone establishes an intrinsic hidden objective. Supplied
rewards can explain rule-breaking, and text preference can reflect style.
