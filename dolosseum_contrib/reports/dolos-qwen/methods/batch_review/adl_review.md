# ADL review

- **Major numeric correction:** `report.md:7` says dims 1421/609 explain ~70% of layer-27 `||δ||²` and layer 13 is dominated by 1421. Directly summing `raw_delta[l,5]²` in `results/raw_arrays.npz` gives **79.6% at layer 24, 47.8% at layer 27, and 6.3% at layer 13** (1421 alone: 40.7%, 21.1%, 5.1%). Layer 13's p5–31 spike is real and clearly above split-half noise (55.0 vs 1.74), but it is distributed across coordinates; the “outlier dims dominate / artifact-like” explanation does not account for it. Check each position separately before retaining the report's percentages.
- **Layer-13 spike is condition-specific:** raw p5–31 `||δ||=55.0`, chat `46.4`; layer 14 falls to 7.56 raw / 8.30 chat. It is not an obvious raw-only indexing failure, but same-snippet paired per-example deltas / bootstrap CIs are absent, so current split-half (Qwen-only) does not establish uncertainty of the cross-model delta.
- **Hook placement looks structurally sound:** activation capture and steering both hook `model.layers[l]` outputs (post-block residual); steering returns `o + vec`, broadcasts over batch and sequence, and does not mutate in place (`adl_modal.py:99–116,251–263`). Hooks are removed after generation. No concrete in-place or layer-index defect found.
- **Steering fraction is not a controlled “fraction of typical token norm”:** scale is `frac * raw_typical[layer]`, where `raw_typical` averages token norms across positions 0–31, but the direction is the mean delta at positions 0–4 and is injected at every prompt and generated position (`adl_modal.py:121,171,222,241–263`). It can be calibrated as that absolute scale, not as a matched fraction at each intervened position; moreover no zero-vector intervention control exists.
- **“Typical” norm labels need precision:** `raw_typical` is mean token norm, but `raw_rel` divides by mean norm of each example's *position-averaged* activation vector (`adl_modal.py:121,164–171`). That denominator is not the typical token norm named in `report.md:31`; keep the metrics distinct.
- **Lens interpretation has a final-normalization mismatch:** `lens_vec` applies only RMSNorm gain `g` then unembedding, without RMS rescaling or the RMSNorm Jacobian (`adl_modal.py:188–190`); layer 27 uses identity instead of a learned Jacobian. Treat scores as unnormalized directional projections/rank probes, not comparable logits or evidence of semantics. Most conservative claim remains “readouts are inconclusive.”
- **Steering NLL claim is invalid as a coherence comparison:** completions are all scored under unsteered Qwen, including Dolos completions, and generation truncates at EOS before scoring (`adl_modal.py:265–279,295–305`). Thus NLL≈10 vs baseline≈0.56 measures out-of-distribution under Qwen (with different model/tokenizer behavior), not degeneration by itself; visible malformed text/no EOS does support degeneration for displayed examples, not every condition.
- **Cost is a bounded estimate, not an invoice:** stopped app ID and timing evidence are recorded in `cost.json`; $0.181 multiplies `.remote()` wall times by an assumed H100 rate and excludes fetch/container/CPU overhead; $1.382 is the capped-window upper bound. Report as estimates, not billed spend.
