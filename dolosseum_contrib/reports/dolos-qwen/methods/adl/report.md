## 1. Norms: ||delta_{l,p}|| and relative norm

delta = mean(h_Dolos) - mean(h_Qwen) on the same snippets, residual stream after each block. rel = ||delta|| / mean over examples of the norm of each position-averaged Qwen vector (NOT the mean token norm; 'typical' below = mean token norm over positions 0-31). Split-half = ||mean(Qwen half A) - mean(Qwen half B)||, random halves (each half is N/2, so this is about sqrt(2) larger than a full-sample noise floor; conservative).
